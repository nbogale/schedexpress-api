📧 Email Template Tester

Test and preview your EJS email templates with beautiful gradients and modern styling.

🚀 Open Template Tester

How to use:

  • Select Template: Choose from General Notification, Grade Notification, or Schedule Change
  • Fill Form: Enter test data for the template variables
  • Render: Click "Render Template" to see the email preview
  • Test Responsive: Resize your browser to test mobile responsiveness

Features:

  • ✅ Real-time EJS template rendering
  • ✅ Beautiful gradient headers and buttons
  • ✅ Color-coded grade status (green/red)
  • ✅ Professional email design
  • ✅ Mobile-responsive preview
